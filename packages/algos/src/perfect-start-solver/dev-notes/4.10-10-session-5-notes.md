# Session 5 — Broader Hard-Board Probe

Micro-session between session 4 and whatever's next. Single scope: address session 4's "matrix-too-narrow" loose end by expanding the board set and probing where random-walk blobs actually struggle, so future comparisons have real signal.

## 1. What we did

- Expanded `compare-blob-sources` board registry from 7 → 19 boards. Additions: 6 unswept tight 25x25/30x30 real boards, 6 simple degenerate-geometry small boards
- Added `--boards <list|all>` and `--rw-only` flags. `--rw-only` skips the generator so a 19-board probe runs in seconds; `--boards` lets sweeps target subsets without editing the file
- Restructured the markdown summary under `--rw-only`: per-board table sorted hardest-first, explicit "candidate hard set" section (random-walk feasibility ≤ 50%)
- No algorithmic work — registry + CLI only, then a random-walk baseline probe followed by a generator verification pass on the hard set

## 2. Results

Ran two passes: first a random-walk-only probe across all 19 boards at two profiles, then a generator verification on the hard set at the profile that produced one.

**Profile sensitivity matters.** At the easier profile (only 2 bursts), random-walk feasibility was near-ceiling across essentially the whole matrix — uninformative, same "matrix-too-narrow" problem as session 4 just on a bigger matrix. Only the harder `[10,7,4,2]` profile produced a hard set. More broadly: profiles with very few or very small bursts are too simplistic to exercise the scorer or the generator. Any future ablation needs realistic profile shapes or it measures nothing, regardless of how hard the boards are.

**Hard-set results at profile `[10,7,4,2]`:**

| board | random-walk | generated | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.21-real-board-tight-corner-1 | 0/4 | 4/20 | gen-only win |
| 3.22-small-corner-pocket | 0/4 | 2/19 | gen-only win |
| pocket-11x11 | 1/4 | 16/20 | gen substantially better |
| pocket-2-11x11 | 2/4 | 19/20 | gen substantially better |
| edge-pocket-2-9x9 | 2/4 | 8/12 | gen modestly better |
| narrow-corridors-11x11 | 1/4 | 4/20 | no gap |

**Headline: 1 → 6 boards where random-walk fails, 1 → 5 boards where the generator is clearly better.**

- **Boards where random-walk struggles** (feasibility ≤ 50%) went from 1 (`tight-corner-1`) to **6**:
  - `3.21-real-board-tight-corner-1`
  - `3.22-small-corner-pocket`
  - `pocket-11x11`
  - `pocket-2-11x11`
  - `edge-pocket-2-9x9`
  - `narrow-corridors-11x11`
- **Boards where the v1 generator is clearly better than random-walk** went from 1 to **5**:
  - **Gen-only wins** (random-walk at 0, generator finds something): `tight-corner-1`, `small-corner-pocket`
  - **Gen substantially better**: `pocket-11x11`, `pocket-2-11x11`
  - **Gen modestly better**: `edge-pocket-2-9x9`
- **`narrow-corridors-11x11`** is the one hard board where the v1 generator doesn't yet open a clear gap — both sources sit at ~20-25%. Given that the v1 scorer/generator are deliberately dumb (session 4 framing), this is expected shape rather than counter-signal: a candidate for exercising smarter work later, not a sign something is broken

A qualitative jump in how much signal the matrix can carry vs session 4's single data point. A nice empirical confirmation of the reframe's direction, though the reframe itself was conceptually motivated from prior brainstorming and not hinging on this result.

## 3. Cautious interpretation

- "Random-walk fails" here is specifically: session-2 reach-more random walk + 4 fixed seeds + profile `[10,7,4,2]`. Different random-walk config, seeds, or profile shapes will shift the set
- 4 random-walk seeds is a small sample; 1/4 or 2/4 results are consistent with a fairly wide true-rate range — treat as "random-walk-hostile at this config," not as calibrated difficulty scores
- The v1 generator is intentionally dumb across scorer, enumeration, and overlap handling. Results below full dominance over random-walk should be read as "where the current baseline sits," not as ceilings
