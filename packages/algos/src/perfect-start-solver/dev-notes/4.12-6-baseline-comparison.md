## Key observations

**Capture quality:** Algo-2 only finds 24 caps on 2 of 8 boards tested. Falls to 23 on most others, and to 22 on 3.22-fairly-open — all boards where algo-1 finds 24 easily.

**Speed:** Orders of magnitude slower across the board — 33x to 33,000x. Even when it finds the same answer (dense-mtns-9x9: both get 24), algo-2 takes 173s vs 7ms.

**Pattern bias:** Algo-1 almost always uses 4-burst patterns starting with burst-12 (`[12, 7, 3, 2]`, `[12, 6, 4, 2]`). Algo-2 always uses 6-burst patterns with smaller bursts. The pipeline gravitates toward many-small-burst configs.

**Lane builder is the bottleneck:** Mappings ≈ lane attempts — nearly every valid burst-to-path mapping produces a lane attempt that fails. The mapper finds plenty of valid assignments, but the greedy lane builder can't construct the required long lanes.

**Timing entry explosion at lower targets:** When algo-2 fails at 24 caps (5,732 timing entries), it descends to 23 caps (50,504 entries — ~9x more) and grinds through all of them.
