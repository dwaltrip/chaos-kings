# Session 3.23-3 — Prefix Enumeration Results

First experiments validating the path prefix structure theory (see `PREFIX-STRUCTURE-THEORY.md`). Two experiments across 46 boards (14 slow, 15 realistic, plus overlap).

Full output saved in `experiments/output/3.23-3-prefix-enumeration-{slow,realistic}.txt` and `experiments/output/3.23-3-solution-spot-check-{slow,realistic}.txt`.

## Key insights

1. **Prefix pools are tiny.** Even degree-4 boards have only ~60 prefixes at depth 4. Degree-2 boards (most slow boards) have ~20. The search space for prefix *selection* is fundamentally small.

2. **A depth-4 prefix narrows the path pool ~20-30x.** On corner-9x9, a single depth-4 prefix selects 1,374 of 36,048 length-12 paths. The current solver searches all 36,048 per burst — prefix selection would scope that down dramatically.

3. **The near-general bottleneck is real, but short.** By depth 3-4, most prefix tips have free≥2 — the path has escaped the constrained zone near the general into open space. The constraint resolution happens in the prefix; the suffix is mostly unconstrained.

4. **Overlap bursts share prefix tiles, not avoid them.** The solution spot-check confirms that 4 of 5 bursts on corner-9x9 go through the same neighbor, diverging at different depths via overlap. Prefix set enumeration must model this coupling, not treat overlap as a separate concern.

5. **Board structure near the general determines prefix behavior, not board size.** corner-9x9 and corner-13x13 have nearly identical prefix data despite 2x size difference. Constrained boards (scattered-pockets, tight-corner) have radically fewer prefixes regardless of total tiles.

**Prefix pools are small.** Degree-2 boards: typically 2 → 4 → ~10 → ~20 across depths 1-4. Degree-4 boards: 4 → ~10 → ~25 → ~60. Even the widest board (open-7x7, degree-4 center) has only 96 at depth 4.

**Extremely constrained boards barely branch.** scattered-pockets stays at 2 prefixes through all depths. edge-pocket-2 goes 2 → 2 → 2 → 3. tight-corner-1 has a dead-end neighbor (one exit leads to a single tile), so it's 2 → 2 → 2 → 4.

**corner-9x9 ≈ corner-13x13.** Nearly identical prefix counts (10 vs 10 at d=3, 24 vs 24 at d=4). The near-general structure is the same; board size only affects the downstream path pool.

## Fan-out narrowing

For each prefix at depth D, how many length-M paths share that prefix. Every long path has exactly one prefix at each depth, so fan-outs across all prefixes sum to total paths.

**corner-9x9 fan-out to length-12 paths (36,048 total):**

| Depth | #Pfx | Min   | Median | Max   | Mean   |
|-------|------|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| 1     | 2    | 18024 | 18024  | 18024 | 18024  |
| 2     | 4    | 8181  | 9012   | 9843  | 9012   |
| 3     | 10   | 1141  | 4351   | 4704  | 3605   |
| 4     | 24   | 467   | 1813   | 2103  | 1502   |

Each depth step roughly partitions the paths into finer buckets. Depth 4 gives a 24x reduction from the full pool (36,048 / 1,502 mean).

**Zero dead prefixes on open/corner boards** — every prefix at every depth can extend to any target length.

**Dead prefixes appear on constrained boards** — pocket-11x11 and pocket-2-11x11 each get 2 dead prefixes at depth 4 (leading into tight pockets). tight-corner-1 has 1 dead prefix at depth 1 (the dead-end neighbor, fan-out = 0 at all target lengths).

**Flat narrowing on corridor boards** — scattered-pockets: fan-out stays at 31 → 31 → 31 → 31 from depth 1 through 4. The corridor is so tight that knowing more of the prefix adds no information. But the path pool is already tiny (62 total), so this isn't a problem.

## Per-neighbor structure

Prefixes group by their first tile (tiles[0] = the neighbor of the general they exit through).

**Symmetric boards split evenly.** corner-9x9: both neighbors get 1 → 2 → 5 → 12 prefixes at each depth. open-7x7: all 4 neighbors get 1 → 3 → 9 → 24.

**Asymmetric boards show clear imbalance.** 3.22-fairly-open: neighbor (8,7) gets 1 → 3 → 9 → 24 while (6,7) gets 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 — one direction is more open. tight-corner-1: one neighbor has 1 → 0 → 0 → 0 (dead end after one tile), the other has 1 → 2 → 2 → 4.

## Tip properties

For each prefix, the "tip" is the last tile. Two metrics: walkable degree (raw neighbor count) and free neighbors (walkable neighbors not in the prefix path or the general).

Free is the actionable metric — it's how many ways the path can actually continue.

**Most prefixes open up by depth 3-4.** Even on corner-9x9 (degree 2 at general): at depth 4, 14 of 24 prefixes have free=3, 8 have free=2, only 2 have free=1. The path has walked away from the constrained corner into open space.

**free=0 is rare.** Only shows up on constrained boards — tight-corner-1 has one at depth 1 (dead-end neighbor), pocket-11x11 gets one at depth 4. These are exactly the "dead" prefixes with zero fan-out.

**free=1 means corridor — forced continuation.** The suffix has no choices, just follows the single available path. Appears occasionally on pocket and edge boards.

**Degree tracks free closely at shallow depths.** At depth 1, free = degree - 1 (the general consumes one neighbor). At deeper depths, free can diverge from degree as the path consumes more neighbors or moves near walls.

## Solution spot-check

For each burst in the solver's solution, how does fan-out narrow from depth 1 through 4?

**corner-9x9** (cap=24, pattern=[10, 5, 4, 3, 2], overlaps=[0, 3, 2, 1, 0]):

Burst 1 (10 captures, overlap=0) — fan-out to length-12:
```
depth 1: 18,024  →  depth 2: 8,181  →  depth 3: 3,477  →  depth 4: 1,374
```
Roughly halving per step. By depth 4, the solution's prefix selects 1,374 of 36,048 paths (26x reduction).

**Overlap bursts share prefixes with earlier bursts.** B1 through B4 all exit through (1,0). They share depth-1 and often depth-2 prefixes, diverging at depth 3-4. B5 is the only burst through the other neighbor (0,1). This matches the theory's prediction about overlap coupling.

**Constrained boards show flat narrowing.** scattered-pockets: both bursts have fan-out 31 at all depths. edge-pocket-2 B3: fan-out stays at 366 from depth 1 through depth 4 (single corridor, no branching).

**Dead-end bursts.** tight-corner-1 B4 sends 1 capture to the dead-end neighbor — TipDeg=1, Free=0, fan-out=0 at all lengths. The solver uses this as a "free" capture that doesn't compete for space.
