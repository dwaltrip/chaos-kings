## What happened

**Post-bugfix profiling baseline and Thread 5 spike (degree-based filter).**

### Profiling baseline

Re-ran deep phase analysis and all-boards solver run to establish post-bugfix (3.22-5) baseline numbers. Categorized all 45 boards into three tiers: fast/low-search (28 boards), higher search/still fast (5 boards), slow/deep search (12 boards). Documented profiling overhead caveat — deep profiling inflates absolute times significantly on high-search-call boards (e.g. corner-7x7: 849ms real vs 3.1s profiled). Counts, percentages, and ratios from deep profiling are reliable; absolute times are not.

Added staleness note to `findings/3.22-3-deep-phase-analysis.md` pointing to the new baseline doc.

### Thread 5 spike: degree-based timing entry filter

**Idea:** Filter timing entries where total zero-overlap bursts exceeds the general's degree. On degree-2 boards (13 of 14 slow boards), this kills 91.6% of entries at cap=24.

**Result:** Correct but ineffective. The filter is strictly weaker than the existing N feasibility check in `searchGrouped`, which runs after B1 and sees fewer available neighbors (B1's path covers neighbor tiles). Every entry killed by the degree filter was already being killed by the in-search N check. Entry counts dropped dramatically (e.g. 36,388 → 11,370 on corner-7x7) but runtimes were flat — confirming the savings is just loop iterations over a trivial check, not real compute.

**Key insight:** The degree filter cannot skip entire capture targets on degree-2 boards. At cap=24, 458 entries (8.4%) have only 2 zero-overlap bursts and survive. Only degree-1 boards (just tight-corner-2) get a full target skip.

Kept in solver with TODO to consider removal. See `findings/3.22-6-degree-filter.md`.
