### Key findings

**Three performance regimes identified:**
1. **Infeasible-target waste** — timing entry explosion. Entries/group: 678 at cap=24 → 3,858 at cap=23 → 8,401 at cap=22 (board-independent, config-determined). Boards: tight-corner-1/2, pocket-11x11, scattered-pockets-13x13, edge-pocket-2, tight-edge-with-chokes.
2. **Deep recursive search** — 90% candidate waste across 465K-815K search calls. Boards: corner-7x7/9x9/13x13, floating-corner-11x11, half-enclosed-half-open, semi-open-with-small-pocket.
3. **Path generation** — 40-280ms dominates trivial search. Boards: open-9x9/11x11/13x13.

**Answers to session 3.22-2 open questions:**
- Q1 (infeasible target cost): Each searchGrouped call feasibility-checks all entries in its group. At cap=23 that's 3,858 entries vs 678 at cap=24 — 5.7x more BigInt popcount work per call, directly explaining the time ratio.
- Q2 (half-enclosed 667ms): 18,748 search calls × ~22 entries/call of feasibility checking. 99.8% killed by neighbor check. Cost is feasibility × entries, not candidate scanning.
- Q3 (90% corner waste): Deep recursion — 471K-815K calls at 3.8-7.6μs each, ~9 candidates scanned per call, ~1 passes.

**Board size is not the driver:** 7 of 9 30x30 boards solve in <55ms. corner-9x9 (81 tiles) takes 5.8s while fairly-open-30x30 (900 tiles) takes 55ms.
