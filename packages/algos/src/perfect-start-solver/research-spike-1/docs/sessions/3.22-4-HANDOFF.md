## Context from session 3.22-4

Session 3.22-4 confirmed that infeasible targets are genuinely infeasible (not solver over-pruning) and killed two naive pre-check approaches. See `sessions/3.22-4-LOG.md` for the full investigation. Key takeaways for Thread 5:

- **Infeasible targets are timing-constrained, not tile-constrained.** Boards have plenty of reachable tiles; the geometry + 50-tick budget make high capture targets impossible. Simple "reachable tiles < target" checks won't fire on any board.
- **At 24 captures, zero timing slack.** Max total overlap = 9 tiles. All entries land at ticks 49-50.
- **The N (neighbor) feasibility check dominates.** 94% of entries on pocket-11x11 fail because they need more zero-overlap bursts than available neighbors. This is detectable before iterating B1 candidates.

## This session's plan: Thread 5

**Goal:** Skip provably infeasible capture targets before entering the B1 candidate loop. 7 of 13 slowSearch boards spend 68-99% of time here.

**Promising approaches:**

1. **Degree-based timing entry filtering.** On degree-2 boards, entries needing 3+ zero-overlap bursts are ALWAYS infeasible. Filter at timing table level. Kills 94% of entries on pocket-11x11. Cheap: count neighbors once, filter once per target.

2. **Root-level feasibility probe with improved distance approximation.** Current `FEASIBILITY_MAX_DIST=4` with crude `beyondTiles = dist - 4` is very rough. Increasing it or improving the estimate tightens the check.

3. **Quick per-target feasibility sweep.** Before iterating B1 candidates, check if ANY timing entry passes feasibility at root level (coveredMask=0). If all fail, skip target.
