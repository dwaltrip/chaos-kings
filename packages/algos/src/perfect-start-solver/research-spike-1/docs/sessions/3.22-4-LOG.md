### pocket-11x11 manual pattern investigation

Built an experiment framework to test hand-designed burst patterns against specific boards. Tested whether pocket-11x11 can achieve 24 captures.

**5 pattern ideas tested**, all following the same structure: B1 goes LEFT then UP to escape the pocket, later bursts use overlap to re-traverse the leftward path before branching to different rows. Variable burst lengths enumerated 0..12.

**Result:** All 5 ideas found many geometrically valid 24-capture combinations, but every one exceeded 50 ticks. The closest was 55 ticks (idea 5). The overlap re-traversal moves consume too many ticks at the 24-capture target, which has zero timing slack (all valid 24-capture entries land at ticks 49-50).

**Also tested pocket-2-11x11** (3-wide mountain wall instead of 4). Same result — solver finds 23 captures.

### Overlap/timing analysis

Investigated the timing table's overlap budget at 24 captures:
- Max total overlap with `maxOverlapPerBurst=3`, `maxBursts=6`: **9 tiles** (14 entries, all at tick 50)
- High-overlap patterns need many small bursts (5-6 bursts like [8,4,4,3,3,2])
- Every 24-capture entry lands at tick 49-50 — zero slack
- The design sketch (with `maxBursts=10`) showed max 17 total overlap, but actual solver config limits it to 9

### Key conclusions

1. **pocket-11x11 at 24 captures is genuinely timing-infeasible** — confirmed by exhaustive search over hand-designed patterns. Not a solver bug or over-pruning.
2. **The feasibility check is correct** — it's properly identifying infeasible targets, not false-pruning.
3. **Thread 5 (capture-target pre-check) remains the right optimization** — skipping infeasible targets cheaply is legitimate.
4. **scattered-pockets-13x13 is NOT tile-count-limited** — it has plenty of reachable tiles, the geometry just makes them hard to capture within the timing constraints. Simple "reachable tiles < target" pre-checks won't work.
5. **Approach #1 (reachable tile count) is dead** — never the binding constraint on any board.
6. **Approach #2 (per-neighbor capacity) collapses to #1** — the union of per-neighbor reachable tiles ≈ total reachable tiles.
