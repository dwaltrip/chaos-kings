**Context:** The research spike has completed initial spikes on two lines of work — neighbor partitioning (L1/L2/L3) and ordering/symmetry (group ordering, symmetry breaking analysis). Key findings: realistic 25x25 boards solve in <500ms, path generation is a significant fixed cost (20-60ms), the solver's main waste on hard boards may be at the capture-target level (exhausting impossible targets before finding solutions at lower capture counts). Symmetry breaking has narrow applicability. Group ordering (fewest-candidates-first) was a negative result.

**This session goals:**

- Read through the findings and session notes for each experiment
- Reflect on what we've learned across the full spike so far
- Re-read the original survey and critical review with fresh eyes, informed by results
- Open-ended discussion and brainstorm about promising next directions
- Consider whether the current solver architecture is hitting fundamental limits or if there are structural improvements worth pursuing
