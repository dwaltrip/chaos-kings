## Preliminary observations

These are initial reads from the data. Deeper analysis deferred.

**Infeasible capture targets dominate tight-corner boards.** On tight-corner-1, 95.5% of time is spent on captures=24 and captures=23 before solving at 22. On tight-corner-2, it's 99.0%. The infeasible targets scan zero inner-loop candidates — all time is in the burst-1 loop × feasibility checking inside `searchGrouped`. This makes thread 5 (capture-target pre-check) the highest-leverage optimization for these boards.

**Candidate scan waste is ~90% on corner boards.** Corner boards (which solve at captures=24, no infeasible-target overhead) show 89.5-90.2% waste — 9 out of 10 candidates fail the overlap check. This is the thread 10 signal for watched literals. 90% waste is meaningful but not the 95%+ that would make it a slam dunk — overhead matters.

**Two distinct performance regimes.** Easy realistic boards are bottlenecked on path gen (39-45ms) with trivial search. Hard boards are bottlenecked on search with trivial path gen. The half-enclosed board is a middle case (18ms path gen, 667ms search). These regimes need different optimizations.

**The tight-corner boards have tiny candidate counts.** tight-corner-1 checks only 66 candidates total (at the successful target). tight-corner-2 checks 13,832. The inner-loop scan waste percentage is high (97-99.5%) but the absolute numbers are small. The bottleneck on these boards is NOT inner-loop scanning — it's the burst-1 iteration across thousands of entries per infeasible target.

## What needs deeper analysis

- Why do infeasible targets cost so much when they scan zero inner-loop candidates? The time is in burst-1 iteration × `searchGrouped` feasibility. How many search calls per infeasible target? What's the feasibility check cost profile at those targets?
- How does half-enclosed-half-open spend 667ms with only 7,244 candidate checks? That's ~92us per check — something else is going on (deep recursion? many search calls with few candidates each?).
- What does the 90% waste on corner boards look like per search depth? Is it uniform or concentrated at specific depths?
- Does the timing table build cost (5-48ms per target) matter? At 3 targets it's ~70ms, small relative to search but not negligible.
